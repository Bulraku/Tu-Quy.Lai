## About Me

Hi, I'm **Tu Quy Lai**, a Game Design student at **Macromedia University of Applied Sciences in Cologne**.

My primary interest lies in game design, particularly gameplay systems, balancing, and player experience design. Alongside these areas, I develop practical skills in 3D asset creation, character modeling, and game art production through both academic and personal projects.

This portfolio currently focuses on my 3D work, showcasing completed projects and works in progress that document my development as a designer and artist. As I continue my studies, I plan to expand the portfolio with additional examples of game design, documentation, and development work.
