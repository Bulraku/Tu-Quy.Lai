## Project Overview

This project is a stylized 3D environment prop based on a cat café window design. The objective was to practice prop modeling, UV mapping, texturing, and presentation workflows using Autodesk Maya and Adobe Substance 3D Painter.

The asset focuses on a cozy, stylized visual style with rounded shapes, pastel colors, wood materials, and decorative cat-themed forms. The goal was to translate the original concept into a readable 3D asset while maintaining its playful and inviting appearance.

## Software Used

- Autodesk Maya

- Adobe Substance 3D Painter

- Arnold Renderer

## Skills Demonstrated

- Environment prop modeling

- UV mapping

- Material creation

- Stylized texturing

- Shape language interpretation

- Asset presentation

## Challenges

One of the primary challenges was translating the 2D concept into a 3D model while preserving the original silhouette and charm. The asset contains several rounded and layered shapes, so maintaining readability from different angles was an important part of the process.

Another challenge involved creating stylized materials that felt distinct while still fitting together as one cohesive prop. The wood, painted surfaces, and metallic accents needed enough variation to be readable without making the asset look too busy.

## Development Process

### Modeling Phase

The window was first modeled in Autodesk Maya, focusing on the main silhouette before refining the arch, pillars, wheels, paw-shaped opening, and decorative details.

![Modeling Phase]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/Cat_Cafe_pics/Starting.png)

### UV Mapping

After the model was completed, UVs were created and organized to prepare the asset for texturing in Substance 3D Painter.

![UV Mapping]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/Cat_Cafe_pics/UV_Mapping.png)

### Texturing Phase

Textures were created in Adobe Substance 3D Painter, with a focus on stylized wood grain, pastel painted surfaces, and warm accent colors.

![Texturing Phase]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/Cat_Cafe_pics/Texturing.png)

### Material Development

Additional material setup and refinement were carried out to improve the final presentation and ensure the asset retained its stylized look under rendered lighting.

![Material Development]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/Cat_Cafe_pics/Hypershade.png)
