## Ashen Knight Dagger Reinterpretation

This project is a 3D recreation inspired by the Ashen Knight Pyke dagger from League of Legends. The goal was to practice hard-surface prop modeling, UV unwrapping, texturing, and presentation workflows using Autodesk Maya and Adobe Substance Painter.

While the original design uses blue spectral energy, I chose to reinterpret the blade with an orange crystal aesthetic as part of an assignment. This artistic decision allowed me to experiment with stylized material creation while maintaining the overall silhouette and identity of the original weapon.



## Software Used
Autodesk Maya
Adobe Substance Painte


## Skills Demonstrated
-Hard-surface modeling

-UV unwrapping

-Texture creation

-Material design

-Asset presentation

### Challenges

One of the main challenges was creating a crystal-like blade material that remained visually readable while preserving the weapon's silhouette. Instead of reproducing the original effect exactly, under permission of my lecturer I exaggerated the crystal structure through stylized crack patterns and emissive-like highlights to emphasize the magical nature of the weapon. preference.
