## Project Overview

This project is a 3D recreation inspired by the Ashen Knight Pyke dagger from League of Legends. The objective was to practice hard-surface prop modeling, UV unwrapping, texturing, and presentation workflows using Autodesk Maya and Adobe Substance 3D Painter.

While the original design features blue spectral energy, I chose to reinterpret the blade using an orange crystal aesthetic as part of an academic assignment. This artistic decision allowed me to experiment with stylized material creation while maintaining the weapon's recognizable silhouette and identity.

## Software Used

- Autodesk Maya
  
- Adobe Substance 3D Painter
  
- Arnold Renderer

## Skills Demonstrated

- Hard-surface modeling
  
- UV unwrapping
  
- Material creation
  
- Stylized texturing
  
- Shader setup
  
- Asset presentation

## Challenges

One of the primary challenges was creating a crystal-like blade material while preserving the readability of the weapon's silhouette. Rather than reproducing the original effect exactly, I explored a stylized approach using exaggerated crystal formations and emissive-inspired highlights.

Another challenge involved configuring the material network for Arnold rendering. Several shader iterations and node adjustments were required to achieve the desired appearance while maintaining compatibility within Maya's rendering pipeline.

## Development Process

### Modeling Phase

The dagger was first modeled in Autodesk Maya, focusing on accurately recreating the overall silhouette before refining individual components such as the blade, guard, eye, and handle.

![Modeling Phase]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/Modeling_StageN%20(1).png)

### Shader Development

Custom materials were assembled and refined within Maya's Hypershade editor. This stage involved building and troubleshooting shader networks to create the final crystal and metallic surface appearance.

![Hypershade Setup]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/Modeling_StageN%20(3).png)
### Early Rendering Tests

Before the final presentation render, multiple Arnold test renders were used to evaluate lighting, material response, and silhouette readability.


![Render Test]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/Modeling_StageN%20(2).png)
## Final Result

The finished asset successfully combines hard-surface modeling techniques with a stylized crystal material treatment. The project served as valuable practice in both technical and artistic aspects of game asset creation.
